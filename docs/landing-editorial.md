# Courtside landing page

Historical landing iteration. Superseded by the approved full visual revamp documented in `DESIGN.md` and `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-21-full-visual-revamp.md`.

The approved Sport Editorial concept supplies the composition: large condensed heading on the left, an app preview over a badminton photograph on the right, a feature strip, and a two-column walkthrough. The existing Court Lines palette remains authoritative: court ink, chalk, court ground and action clay. Chartreuse from the concept is replaced with clay.

The landing preview renders the existing `MatchCourt` and `ParticipantGroup` components with local fictional data. It stays read-only and makes no club or account requests. Sample tab labels are explanatory, not navigation controls. Signup, sign-in and explicit club URLs keep their existing behavior.

Barlow Condensed 800 is self-hosted for landing headings only; its SIL Open Font License is included with the font. App typography and operational components are unchanged. The generated badminton photograph is an illustrative asset, not evidence of a particular club or customer.

Copy is edited using blader/humanizer guidance: concrete actions, ordinary Indonesian, no fabricated proof, no guaranteed fairness or fully automatic operation claims. The walkthrough reflects admin check-in and fees set at session close. Members do not self check in.
