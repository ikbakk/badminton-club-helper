# Players, History, Fund & Settings

## Players public

Show permanent roster. Player detail can later show set W/L, rating, today's movement, recent sessions. Do not expose uncertainty. Guests do not clutter main roster.

## Players admin

Add/edit/deactivate member, promote guest, later initialize/correct rating. Keep roster management distinct from courtside check-in.

## History

Replace placeholder after close flow works:

```text
27 Sep
11 players • 24 sets • 3h 04m
```

Detail: participants, matches/sets, time, fee, share recap. No charts required.

## Fund public

Aggregate transparency only:

- accumulated fund,
- received,
- court expense,
- shuttlecock expense,
- other expense.

Never expose individual debts publicly.

Until finance exists, retain honest empty state rather than demo data.

## Settings

Permanent-admin area:

- club name/logo,
- payment destinations,
- admin/finance authority,
- PIN policy if applicable,
- account/password,
- sign out.

Do not move live-session controls into Settings.
